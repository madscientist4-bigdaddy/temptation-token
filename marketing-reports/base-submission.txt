Project name: Temptation Token ($TTS)

Tagline (56 chars): Hot-or-Not on Base — vote $TTS, top voter wins the pool.

One-liner:
A weekly on-chain "Hot or Not" voting game. Players vote $TTS on profiles; the
top voter and the winning profile split the prize pool, 10% goes to an
anti-trafficking charity, and every losing vote is burned. Provably fair via
Chainlink VRF; fully settled on Base mainnet.

- TTSVotingV3d voting contract, Chainlink-automated weekly rounds (Keeper3).

- Prize split 35% top voter / 35% winning profile / 10% charity / 20% house.
- App: https://app.temptationtoken.io (Vite SPA + Vercel serverless).

Why Base:
- Cheap, fast settlement makes micro-stakes voting viable every week.
- Chainlink VRF + Automation on Base gives provably-fair, hands-off rounds.
- Onchain burns tie supply directly to engagement.
