How topping up actually works

$TTS is not listed on any card provider, so no service sells it to you directly. Anyone claiming otherwise is not selling you $TTS. In the app you will see two honest routes:

  • Swap — if you already hold ETH or USDC on Base, exchange it for $TTS at a live quote.
  • Card — a payment provider sells you ETH or USDC into your own wallet, and you then swap. Two steps, shown plainly.

The app refuses any purchase that would move the $TTS price by more than 5%, and tells you why. That limit exists to stop you overpaying on a thin market — we would rather sell you nothing than sell you a bad trade.
