## Full description
**What you're buying.** A complete, deployed, self-operating product. Not a prototype and
not a codebase — a live system on Base mainnet that has run 13 weekly rounds and settled
12 of them onchain.

**How it works.** Creators submit a profile. Fans vote with $TTS. Each round runs Monday
to Sunday on a fixed calendar anchor, so end times never drift. At close, Chainlink VRF
picks the winning profile weighted by votes; the contract splits the pool (35% top voter,
35% winning profile, 10% to an anti-trafficking nonprofit, 20% house), mints three Trophy
NFTs, and burns the losing votes. Every creator profile links out to her own link hub.

**Included:** the LLC or its assets, domains, brand, full codebase, contract admin
(transferred via the 2-of-2 Safe), treasury allocation, apps and builds, brand channels
subject to each platform's rules, and 30 days of transition support.

**Built and live:** React web app on Vercel · Android build · Telegram bot and channel ·
Face ID sign-up with gas-free wallets · card purchases via Transak · club partner
onboarding · referral engine with anti-sybil checks (self-referral, double-capture,
referrer-hijack and funding-source all verified rejecting in production) · admin
dashboard · automated content publishing and SEO site.

**Runs itself.** Rounds 9 through 12 each settled within 30 minutes of close with no human
involvement — four consecutive clean rollovers. Settlement, VRF funding and health
monitoring are automated, with Telegram alerts and a public read-only status endpoint.
