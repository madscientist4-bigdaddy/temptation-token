How Does Temptation Token Work?

Temptation Token runs on the Base blockchain — Coinbase's Layer 2 network offering near-zero transaction fees and instant confirmations. Each week, approved profiles compete in a voting round. Players vote TTS to support their favorite — each TTS voted equals one lottery ticket.

At round close, a Chainlink VRF provably fair random draw selects the winner weighted by ticket count. Big spenders have better odds. Small players always have a shot. The house never manipulates the outcome — it's on-chain and verifiable by anyone.

When do rounds start and end? +

Rounds run Monday 12:00 AM EDT → Sunday 11:59 PM EDT — exactly one week. Settlement via Chainlink VRF happens automatically within minutes of round close. A new round begins immediately after.

Note: The schedule is EDT (UTC−4). During winter (November–March), clocks shift to EST (UTC−5), causing a 1-hour drift due to Chainlink's UTC-only cron system.

Is there a transfer tax on $TTS? +
