App name: Temptation Token: Vote & Win
Short description (80 max): Vote for your favorites each week in a provably-fair
on-chain contest.
Full description:
Temptation Token is a free-to-play weekly voting game built on the Base network.
Browse each week's lineup, cast votes for your favorites, and follow a transparent,
provably-fair contest that settles automatically on-chain.

HOW IT WORKS
- New players get a free token bonus just for signing in — no purchase required.
- Vote for profiles you like during the weekly round.
- Winners are selected by a verifiable random draw (Chainlink VRF) — no one can rig
  the outcome.
- Every result and payout is recorded on-chain and publicly verifiable.

WHY PLAYERS LOVE IT
- Provably fair — the draw is transparent and tamper-proof.
- A fresh round every week.
- Powered by Base for near-instant, near-zero-fee transactions.
- Watch results settle live on the blockchain.

Temptation Token is a game of community voting and on-chain transparency. Tokens are
in-game utility for voting. Play free, vote weekly, follow the results in real time.

18+. Play responsibly.
