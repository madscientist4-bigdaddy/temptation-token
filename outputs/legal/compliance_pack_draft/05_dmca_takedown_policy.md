## 3. Counter-notice
The affected user may submit a counter-notice (§512(g)) containing their signature, identification of the removed material and its former location, a statement under penalty of perjury of good-faith belief the removal was a mistake/misidentification, their contact information, and consent to jurisdiction of `[the appropriate federal court]`. If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may restore the material in 10–14 business days unless the complainant files suit.
