## 1. Minimum age
The Service is for **adults 18 years or older** (or older where local law requires). No one under 18 may access, submit content to, appear in content on, vote in, or transact on the Service.

## 2. How age is enforced
1. **Attestation.** Every user must affirmatively acknowledge they are 18+ (recorded with timestamp).
2. **Identity verification for anyone who submits a photo.** Before a profile is published, the submitter must provide a **government-issued photo ID** showing they are 18+ **and** a **dated selfie holding that ID**. An administrator reviews both and **compares the selfie to the ID** to confirm the submitter is the adult depicted and is the same person.
3. **No third parties without proof.** Content depicting anyone other than the verified submitter is not permitted unless `[a signed model release + that person's ID/age proof is provided — confirm process]`.
4. **Ongoing enforcement.** We may re-verify, suspend, or remove any account or content at any time. Suspected minors are removed immediately and reported where required.

## 3. Consent to depiction and use
By submitting content, the submitter confirms, and (where applicable) each depicted person confirms, that they:
- are **18+**;
- **consent** to the photo's collection, public display in the game, and use to operate and promote the Service;
- **separately opt in** (or not) to use of their **likeness in commemorative NFTs** — off by default;
- understand the content will be **publicly visible** and that **on-chain records are permanent** and cannot be deleted.

Consent may be **withdrawn** for future use by contacting `[privacy@temptationtoken.io]`; withdrawal stops future display but **cannot reverse immutable on-chain records or already-minted NFTs**. `[Counsel: reconcile with any perpetual-license language in the ToS/rights grant.]`
