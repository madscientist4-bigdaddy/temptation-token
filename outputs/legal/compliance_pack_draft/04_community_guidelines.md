**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` · Blockchain Entertainment LLC

These Guidelines apply to all content and conduct on the Service (profiles, votes, chat, Telegram/social channels operated by us). They supplement the Terms of Service.

## 1. Who can appear
- Only **verified adults (18+)** may appear in submitted photos. Submit photos **of yourself** unless you have a signed release and age proof for every other person shown.
- No minors, ever — in any part of an image, background included.

## 2. Content standards (SFW)
Permitted: tasteful, clothed, **safe-for-work** photos of yourself.
**Not permitted:**
- Nudity, sexually explicit or pornographic content, or content whose primary purpose is sexual gratification.
- Non-consensual, hidden-camera, deepfake, or AI-generated impersonation content.
- Content depicting or promoting **sexual exploitation, trafficking, or coercion**.
- Violence, gore, hate symbols, harassment, or content targeting protected classes.
- Illegal content, drugs/weapons sales, or promotion of illegal activity.
- Personal data of others (doxxing), or contact info that facilitates off-platform solicitation.
- Spam, scams, phishing, fake giveaways, or misleading financial claims.
- Copyright/trademark infringement (see DMCA/Takedown Policy).

## 3. Conduct
- No harassment, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse of other users.
- No **vote manipulation**: no bots, sybil/multi-account voting, wash-voting, self-referral abuse, or collusion to game pools or rewards.
- No attempts to exploit smart contracts, bypass verification/geoblocking, or defraud the pool, charity, or other users.
- No impersonation of the Company, staff, or others.

## 4. Financial-content rules
This is an entertainment game, not investment. Do not present $TTS, voting, staking, or payouts as guaranteed income or investment advice. Do not shill, pump-and-dump, or make false claims in our channels.

## 5. Enforcement
We may, at our discretion and without notice: remove content; withhold or claw back ineligible rewards; suspend or ban accounts; and report illegal conduct to authorities. Serious violations (minors, non-consensual or trafficking content) result in immediate removal and reporting.

## 6. Reporting
Report violations to `[support@temptationtoken.io]` (or the in-app/Telegram report channel). Copyright issues: see the DMCA/Takedown Policy. Non-consensual intimate imagery or suspected minors: `[dedicated urgent channel/email]` — handled as priority.
