How we protect people

This page describes, factually, what we actually do today to keep the platform safe and age-appropriate. It is a description of our current practices, not a legal statement.

🪪 Every submitter is identity-verified

Before a profile goes live, the person who submits it must provide a photo of a government-issued ID and a selfie holding that ID with today's date written on paper. Our team reviews the ID, the selfie, and the submitted profile photo together and confirms they are the same person before approving.

🔞 18+ only

Submitters must be verified as 18 or older from their government ID. Everyone who connects a wallet must also acknowledge they are 18+ before participating.

✅ Consent is required at submission

To submit a profile, the person must affirmatively check a consent box agreeing that their submitted photo may be used in stylized commemorative NFTs awarded to round participants. The box must be ticked to submit — it is not optional.

🔒 ID documents are stored privately and access-logged

Government IDs and verification selfies are stored in a private storage bucket that is not publicly accessible. They are viewable only by an authenticated administrator, through short-lived links, and every view of an ID or selfie is logged (which document, which reviewer session, and when). ID documents are retained for compliance and verification recordkeeping.

📩 Takedown & questions

To request a takedown, ask a question about your data, or report a concern, contact support@temptationtoken.io. Note that transactions recorded on the Base blockchain are public and permanent and cannot be deleted.

This page reflects current operational practice and may be updated as our processes evolve. It does not constitute a legal representation about compliance with any specific law.
