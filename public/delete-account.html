Delete your account and data

Temptation Token (io.temptationtoken.app) identifies you by your wallet address. You can delete your data yourself, from inside the app, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Temptation Token.
  2. Tap your wallet / Connect button in the top bar.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Review exactly what is and is not deleted, then sign the confirmation message with your wallet. Signing proves you own the address — it costs no gas and moves no funds.

Or by email

Email support@temptationtoken.io from any address, telling us the wallet address to delete. We may ask you to prove control of it before we act, because a wallet address is public and anyone could otherwise request deletion of someone else's data. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile submissions — photo, display name and links
  • Your identity verification record
  • Your government ID and selfie images
  • Your 18+ acknowledgement
  • Any linked-wallet records

What cannot be deleted

On-chain votes, payouts and trophiesThese are public blockchain records on Base. They are permanent and outside our control — nobody can delete them, including us.
Bonus claim recordsRetained indefinitely for fraud prevention, as stated in our Privacy Policy.

Reporting content

To report a profile you believe breaks our rules, open it in the app and tap ⚑ Report this entry, or email support@temptationtoken.io. Every entry is reviewed by a moderator before it appears, and reports are reviewed again on receipt.
