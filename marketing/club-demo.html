For clubs

Send us your creators. Earn 10% of every pot they win — paid straight to your wallet, on-chain.

1

You get a club code

A short word — VIXENS, DOLLHOUSE, whatever you like. You hand it to your creators.

2

Your creators enter it when they submit

When we approve a profile carrying your code, the smart contract links it to your wallet on-chain. No spreadsheets, no chasing.

3

They win → you get paid, automatically

If a club-linked profile wins the week, the contract sends 10% of the pot to your wallet in the same settlement transaction. You never invoice anyone.
